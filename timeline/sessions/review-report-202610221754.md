## Loop 2 — logic and upstream compliance

1. **Blocking, fixed.** The architecture put `overflow-x: clip` on the body "as a guard, tested at
   375 px". The guard makes the REQ-16 test unable to fail: an overflowing element would be hidden,
   and the document would still be exactly as wide as the viewport. Probe reasoning: plant a 600 px
   wide element at 375 px; with the clip, `scrollWidth == clientWidth` → MISSED; without it →
   CAUGHT. Removed the guard and wrote down why.
2. **Blocking, fixed.** REQ-9 says the build fails when a part exceeds its length limit, and REQ-6
   limits the paper summary to 2–4 sentences, but the content-model table had no rule for the
   summary. Added.
3. **Blocking, fixed.** "One setting" (REQ-15) was not true as written: `BASE_PATH` and
   `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_PATH` were two variables that could disagree. `next.config.ts` now copies the
   one into the other.
4. Checked, no change: the Timeline lives in the root layout and reads the active slug with
   `useSelectedLayoutSegment()`; the card lives in the page under `template.tsx`, so the Timeline
   keeps its scroll position while the card re-mounts and animates. Consistent with REQ-2 and REQ-8.
